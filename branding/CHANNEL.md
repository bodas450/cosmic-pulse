## Description (YouTube "About")

Watch the universe move to the beat.

Cosmic Pulse turns the story of space into cinematic 3D music videos. Stars are born from cold clouds of gas, giants collapse and explode as supernovae, black holes swallow light, and galaxies spin into shape, with every cut, flash and explosion timed to the rhythm of the music.

No narration, no filler. Just the cosmos and a soundtrack, in sync.

🌌 What you'll find here:
• The life and death of stars, from nebula to black hole
• How the Solar System, the Moon and the planets formed
• Galaxies, the Big Bang and the fate of the universe
• Supernovae, neutron stars, pulsars and black holes
• Epic, ambient and cinematic electronic music

New space music videos every week. Turn the sound up, go full screen, and enjoy the ride.

🔔 Subscribe and let the universe set the beat.
